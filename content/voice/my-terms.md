# My Terms

This is the core proprietary vocabulary for my content.

These terms should appear repeatedly across videos, captions, carousels,
long-form content, and product narratives so the audience gradually
associates the concepts with my brand.

The goal is not to sound complicated. The goal is to create **memorable
language for ideas I repeatedly teach**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Time-Maxing

**Meaning:** Intentionally getting more meaningful value out of the
limited time available to you.

Time-maxing is not simply about doing more work. It means using your
time deliberately across the things that matter: meaningful work,
learning, health, relationships, hobbies, experiences, and rest.

**Core idea:**

> You do not need more than 24 hours. You need to use your 24 hours
> better.

**Example script line:**

> "I don't believe in working every waking hour. I believe in
> Time-Maxing."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Attention Budget

**Meaning:** Treating attention as a limited resource that should be
deliberately allocated.

Just as money has a budget, attention should have one too.

The question is not only:

> "How much time do I have?"

It is also:

> "Where am I spending my limited attention?"

**Example script line:**

> "Your time budget might be 24 hours, but your attention budget is much
> smaller."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Attention Tax

**Meaning:** The hidden cognitive cost created by distractions,
notifications, context switching, and constantly interrupted attention.

The visible distraction may last only a few minutes, while the loss of
focus can affect the quality of the work that follows.

**Example script line:**

> "The problem with your phone isn't only the 20 minutes you lose. It's
> the Attention Tax you pay afterward."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. AI Leverage

**Meaning:** Using AI to multiply what you can accomplish rather than
using it merely as a shortcut.

AI leverage means giving AI appropriate parts of the workflow while
retaining human judgment, direction, verification, creativity, and
decision-making.

**Example script line:**

> "The future isn't about working harder. It's about learning how to use
> AI leverage."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. AI Amplification

**Meaning:** Using AI to strengthen an existing human capability instead
of replacing that capability entirely.

AI should amplify thinking, research, writing, analysis,
experimentation, and execution.

It should not become an excuse to stop thinking independently.

**Example script line:**

> "Use AI for amplification, not intellectual outsourcing."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Friction Design

**Meaning:** Deliberately increasing friction around behaviours you want
to reduce and decreasing friction around behaviours you want to
encourage.

Examples:

-   Block distracting apps.
-   Put the phone away before deep work.
-   Keep gym clothes ready.
-   Prepare study materials in advance.
-   Remove unnecessary steps from important routines.

**Core idea:**

> Make the right behaviour easier and the wrong behaviour harder.

**Example script line:**

> "You don't always need more discipline. Sometimes you need better
> Friction Design."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Discipline Debt

**Meaning:** The accumulated consequences of repeatedly postponing
behaviours you know matter.

Small acts of avoidance can accumulate.

Examples include repeatedly skipping exercise, postponing important
study, avoiding difficult tasks, or continuously choosing distraction
over deliberate action.

**Example script line:**

> "Every time you keep postponing something important, you may be adding
> to your Discipline Debt."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Skill Stacking

**Meaning:** Developing multiple complementary skills so that they
reinforce one another.

Instead of relying on a single capability, build combinations.

For example:

> Medicine + research + communication + AI + writing

The combination can create a distinctive capability profile.

**Example script line:**

> "You don't need to be the best in the world at one thing. Start Skill
> Stacking."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Polymath Stack

**Meaning:** Your personal combination of knowledge and skills across
multiple domains.

The Polymath Stack is the broader identity-level version of Skill
Stacking.

It represents the deliberate construction of a multidisciplinary
capability set.

**Example script line:**

> "Your degree is one layer of your identity. Your Polymath Stack is
> everything else you choose to build."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. Ambition Gap

**Meaning:** The distance between the life you currently live and the
life you believe you could build.

The Ambition Gap can come from:

-   lack of skills
-   poor execution
-   distraction
-   inconsistent habits
-   fear of failure
-   comfort
-   lack of clarity

**Example script line:**

> "Your problem might not be a lack of ambition. It might be the size of
> your Ambition Gap."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11. Comfort Tax

**Meaning:** The future cost of repeatedly choosing short-term comfort
over long-term growth.

Comfort can feel cheap today while becoming expensive later.

Examples:

-   avoiding difficult learning
-   choosing scrolling instead of important work
-   refusing to leave the comfort zone
-   consistently choosing convenience over growth

**Example script line:**

> "Comfort feels free today, but you might be paying a Comfort Tax years
> from now."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 12. Energy-Maxxing

**Meaning:** Deliberately managing physical, mental, and emotional
energy so that your best energy is directed toward your highest-value
activities.

Time management asks:

> "Where is my time going?"

Energy-maxxing asks:

> "Where is my best energy going?"

It recognizes that two hours of highly focused, high-energy work are not
necessarily equivalent to two hours performed while exhausted and
distracted.

**Example script line:**

> "I care more about Energy-Maxxing than squeezing another two hours
> into my day."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# How to Use These Terms in Scripts

## 1. Build the vocabulary around recurring problems

Do not force a term into every video.

Instead, connect each concept to the problem it explains.

### Time problem

Use:

**Time-Maxing**

### Distraction problem

Use:

**Attention Budget → Attention Tax**

### AI workflow problem

Use:

**AI Leverage → AI Amplification**

### Habit/discipline problem

Use:

**Friction Design → Discipline Debt**

### Career/learning problem

Use:

**Skill Stacking → Polymath Stack**

### Ambition problem

Use:

**Ambition Gap → Comfort Tax**

### Energy problem

Use:

**Energy-Maxxing**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Define a new term the first few times you use it

When introducing a concept, do not assume the audience already
understands it.

Use a simple structure:

> **Term → simple definition → example → takeaway**

Example:

> "I call this the Attention Tax. It's the hidden cost you pay after
> every distraction. You check Instagram for ten minutes, but the real
> cost is the focus you lose when you return to work."

Once the audience understands the term, you can use it without
explaining it every time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Use the terms as hooks

Your proprietary vocabulary should help create recognizable hooks.

Examples:

> "You're not bad at time management. You're bad at Time-Maxing."

> "You don't have an attention problem. You have an Attention Budget
> problem."

> "The hidden cost of your phone is the Attention Tax."

> "Stop asking AI to do the work. Start building AI Leverage."

> "Your environment is stronger than your willpower. Learn Friction
> Design."

> "Every ignored responsibility creates Discipline Debt."

> "Stop collecting random hobbies. Build a Polymath Stack."

> "Your biggest problem might be the Ambition Gap."

> "Comfort isn't free. It comes with a Comfort Tax."

> "Stop optimizing your schedule and start Energy-Maxxing."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Turn individual terms into recurring series

A term becomes powerful when the audience sees it repeatedly.

Possible recurring series:

### Time-Maxing

-   "3 Time-Maxing rules"
-   "Time-Maxing my week"
-   "Time-Maxing as a medical student"
-   "Time-Maxing without working 14 hours"

### Attention Budget / Attention Tax

-   "Protect your Attention Budget"
-   "Where your Attention Tax is coming from"
-   "How I reduced my Attention Tax"

### AI Leverage / AI Amplification

-   "AI Leverage for students"
-   "How I use AI to amplify my workflow"
-   "AI Leverage instead of AI dependence"

### Friction Design / Discipline Debt

-   "Friction Design 101"
-   "How I use friction to beat distraction"
-   "Are you accumulating Discipline Debt?"

### Skill Stacking / Polymath Stack

-   "Build your Polymath Stack"
-   "My current Skill Stack"
-   "Why ambitious students should Skill Stack"

### Ambition Gap / Comfort Tax

-   "Close your Ambition Gap"
-   "The hidden Comfort Tax"
-   "Comfort vs ambition"

### Energy-Maxxing

-   "Energy-Maxxing my study day"
-   "Why Energy-Maxxing beats time management"
-   "Protect your highest-energy hours"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Create contrasts

Contrasts make these concepts easier to remember.

Examples:

> **Time management vs Time-Maxing**

> **Willpower vs Friction Design**

> **Screen time vs Attention Tax**

> **AI dependence vs AI Leverage**

> **Random skills vs a Polymath Stack**

> **Short-term comfort vs Comfort Tax**

> **More hours vs Energy-Maxxing**

These comparisons can become entire short-form videos.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Make the terminology feel natural

The goal is not to say:

> "Here is another proprietary framework I have invented."

Instead, speak as though these are natural concepts in your worldview.

For example:

> "I've started thinking about productivity less as time management and
> more as Time-Maxing."

That feels conversational.

Then repeat the term across future content until the audience begins
using it themselves.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Do not overload a single script

Use **one primary proprietary term per short-form video** whenever
possible.

A second term can support it, but too many coined concepts at once will
make the message feel artificial.

A useful structure:

**Hook** → **one core term** → **explanation** → **real example** →
**action** → **CTA**

Example:

> "Your phone isn't stealing just your time. It's charging you an
> Attention Tax."
>
> Explain the concept.
>
> Show a real example.
>
> Give one practical intervention.
>
> "That's why I use Focalyst to add friction between me and distracting
> apps."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Connect the vocabulary to Focalyst naturally

Focalyst should become the practical implementation layer of these
ideas.

Examples:

**Time-Maxing** → planning

**Attention Budget** → deciding what deserves attention

**Attention Tax** → identifying distraction

**Friction Design** → app blocking

**Energy-Maxxing** → scheduling demanding work around energy

**Discipline Debt** → tracking consistency

The product should demonstrate the philosophy rather than interrupting
the educational content with an unrelated advertisement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# The vocabulary flywheel

The long-term goal is:

**Coin the concept** → **Define it** → **Teach it** → **Use it
repeatedly** → **Show it in your own life** → **Turn it into a
framework** → **Attach it to a product or resource** → **Let the
audience start using the term**

The strongest sign that a term has become associated with the brand is
when people start using it without being prompted.

These terms should reinforce one larger message:

> **Own your time. Protect your attention. Manage your energy. Use AI as
> leverage. Build discipline through systems. Stack skills. Think
> bigger.**

That is the vocabulary system behind the brand.
